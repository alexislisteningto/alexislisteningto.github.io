# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Instagram and dating-app visitors arriving on a phone, plus friends who want a quick view of Alex's listening habits or a way to send a music recommendation.

## Product Purpose

Turn current listening, recent listening, and long-term taste into a compact personal introduction. Success means a visitor understands the joke immediately, scrolls into the music rabbit hole, and can send one thoughtful recommendation.

## Brand Personality

Handmade, candid, and funny. The site should feel like a personal homepage someone kept alive because it still says more than a polished profile template.

## Anti-references

Modern SaaS landing pages, portfolio templates, glassmorphism, gradients, rounded card grids, startup copy, cinematic scroll effects, and any redesign that sanitizes the Web 1.0 character.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve the handmade evidence. Comic Sans, native controls, hard edges, and slightly awkward directness are identity.
2. Lead with the live fact. The current track owns the first viewport without explanatory copy.
3. Reward the scroll. Recent listening is the visual hit; the top 100 is the rabbit hole.
4. Let data stay honest. Repeated recent albums remain repeated, and Last.fm-derived lifetime rankings are labeled by context rather than editorialized.
5. Hide infrastructure, not personality. Loading, errors, abuse controls, and secret handling should be reliable and quiet.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast and keyboard behavior. Preserve browser zoom, use visible labels and focus states, support reduced motion, provide useful image alternatives, and keep every interaction usable without hover.
